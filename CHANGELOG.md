# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `skeleton_error_report` (new package, in every project): errors from the device reach the backend. `installErrorReporting` chains `FlutterError.onError` and `PlatformDispatcher.instance.onError`, `runReportedZoned` guards the zone of `main`, and an `ErrorReporter` (interface — a service such as Sentry can replace the default) gets them. `ServerErrorReporter` posts to the kotlin-skeleton `alert` module's `POST /api/v1/client-errors` with the screen, the app version and the trace id of the last API request; an equal error is folded into a `count` (the first goes at once), a run sends at most 20, and a failing or offline send is dropped without touching the app (no queue). The starter wires it behind `ERROR_REPORT=true` (and `APP_RELEASE` for the version) — off by default, like the server path (`skeleton.alert.client-errors.enabled`); `apps/starter/lib/main.dart` now builds a `ProviderContainer` + `UncontrolledProviderScope` instead of `ProviderScope` so the reporter can read the router and the client.
- `skeleton_api_client`: every request carries a W3C `traceparent` and `X-Trace-Id` (a `traceparent` the caller set is kept), and `ApiClient.lastTraceId` is the trace id of the latest request.

### Fixed

- `skeleton_iap`: `iapEntitlementsProvider` and `iapSubscriptionProvider` read again when the app comes back to the front and the last read is older than `iapResumeRefreshAfterProvider` (5 minutes), so a refund or an immediate cancel brings the ads back without a restart.
- `skeleton_push`: "Open settings" uses `permission_handler`'s `openAppSettings` instead of `app_settings` (SwiftPM only, which broke CocoaPods apps such as `apps/sample`).
- `apps/starter` (`--packages push`): the `PushPrompt` band is no longer drawn on the first screen right after sign-in (nothing valuable has happened yet); the notification settings entry stays and its switch asks for the permission when the person chooses it. The Android manifest already carries `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` for `push` (a stamp test checks it).
- `skeleton_board`: sending or deleting a comment reads the post list again, so the list's comment count is not stale under the detail screen.

### Added

- `skeleton_legal` consent history (twin of the history in the web `ConsentSettings`): `LegalApi.history` for `GET /legal/consents/me/history`, `ConsentEvent`, `ConsentHistoryView` (title, agreed or withdrawn with the time, version, "Load more", retry) and its `LegalLabels` (with `.ko()`). The starter's account settings open it at `/account/consents`. The per-document state and the switch for an optional consent of the web screen are not built.
- `skeleton_push`: after the system refused notifications, `PushPrompt` and `PushSettingsView` show an "Open settings" button (`PushLabels.openSettings`, Korean `설정 열기`) that opens the app's settings page through `permission_handler`'s `openAppSettings` (works with CocoaPods and SwiftPM; 12.x because 13 needs compileSdk 37, above the Flutter default) (`pushOpenSettingsProvider`); returning to the app with the permission given registers the device (the controller reads the permission again on resume while denied).
- `skeleton_audio` (`--packages audio`): background music and sound effects over `audioplayers` behind a fakeable `AudioEngine`. Each sound is on or off with a volume 0..1, kept on the device (`AudioSettingsController`, `savedAudioSettingsProvider`); `AudioSettingsSection` (switch and slider each, `AudioLabels.ko()`); `Audio` (`playBgm` loops and changes track with a short fade, `stopBgm`, `playSfx` overlapping) that sends nothing to the player while a sound is off or at 0, pauses the music while the app is in the background and resumes it. iOS `ambient` (silent switch, mixes with other music), Android game usage without the audio focus. The starter gets the settings section in the account screen only and plays nothing. Catalog entry `audio`.
- `skeleton_storage` `HeldImage`: a `StoredImage` that keeps the picture it has drawn until a new signed address is loaded, so a picture whose address is asked again does not blink to the waiting box.
- Data of one person is dropped with the session: `skeleton_auth` `sessionResetOf({name: provider})` invalidates the listed providers when a session ends, begins or one account is swapped for another. The starter lists its own (`userScopedProviders`) or exempts them with a reason (`sessionScopeExempt`) in `session_scope.dart`, and `test/session_scope_test.dart` fails when a provider in `lib/` (public, or private and kept alive) is in neither.
- `skeleton_video` (`--packages video`, twin of react `video`): video upload straight to the storage in parts (`uploadVideo`: three parts at a time, each tried four times with a fresh address, resume from the parts the storage holds, complete with the ETags), `VideoApi` (`/videos/*`), `dioPartPut`, and the playlist choice (`playbackUrls`, `grantExpired`, `afterFailure`, `resolveVideoUrl`). `uploadVideo` takes a `cancelToken` (`UploadCancelled`), refuses a resume session of another size (`VideoUploadSession.bytes`, `ResumeFileMismatchError`) and signs the part addresses a few at a time and again when ten minutes old. No player widget. `skeleton_storage` `StorageApi.images(keys)` for `POST /storage/images` (`StoredImageInfo`: conversion state and signed `thumb` / `display` addresses). Catalog entry `video`.
- Ad removal by a monthly subscription as well as the once-bought product: `skeleton_iap` `iapSubscriptionProvider` (account's subscription, `404` = none, re-read after a grant and on resume after a failure) and `IapSubscription.keepsOn(planCode, now)` (`ACTIVE` / `CANCELING` of the plan with the period not over). The starter's `adsRemovedFor` takes `planCode` (`ADS_REMOVAL_PLAN_CODE`, empty = subscriptions do not remove ads): bought OR subscribed, off while reading, shown on a failed read. `apps/sample` removes ads for the once-bought `ads-removal` product or a running `standard` plan.
- `skeleton_ads`: `adsRemovedProvider` (default `false`, the app overrides it): when true the banner is not drawn and no interstitial is loaded or shown; rewarded ads, the consent flow and the privacy button are unchanged. `skeleton_ads` does not know `skeleton_iap`.
- `skeleton_iap`: products bought once and kept. `IapProduct.consumable` (from `GET /iap/config`, default `true`); a `consumable: false` product is bought as non-consumable and never consumed on Android (only acknowledged), so it cannot be bought twice and shows in a restore; a purchase arriving at start reads the product map first and stays open if that fails. `IapApi.entitlements()`, `IapEntitlements.owns(itemType, itemRef)` and `iapEntitlementsProvider` (read again after every grant, none when signed out) for `GET /iap/entitlements` of the kotlin `payment-iap` module.
- `skeleton_ads`: interstitial and rewarded ads on the same `AdsSdk` boundary. `interstitialAdProvider(unitId)` keeps one loaded and `showIfAllowed()` holds back until `interstitialMinIntervalProvider` (60 s) has passed since the last close or the first load; `rewardedAdProvider((unitId:, ssvUserId:))` with `RewardedStatus`, `show()` giving an `AdReward?` only when the SDK reported it earned, and `ssvUserId` sent for server-side verification; `AdUnitIds.interstitial` / `.rewarded`; `FakeAdsSdk` can fail loads or shows and choose the reward. Catalog: `ads` now lists the optional kotlin `ads-admob` module (the SSV receiver). `apps/sample` shows one interstitial (leaving a post) and one rewarded button (settings).
- `skeleton_ads` (`--packages ads`): Google AdMob banner behind a fakeable `AdsSdk` boundary: UMP consent (update, form, `canRequestAds`) before one SDK start (`adsControllerProvider`), `AdBanner` (anchored adaptive, empty until ready), `AdsPrivacyOptionsButton` (only where the consent entry is required), `AdUnitIds.banner` (real unit only in a release build, Google's test unit otherwise), `AdsLabels` with `.ko()`, `FakeAdsSdk`. Banner only; no interstitial or rewarded ads.
- `koBoardReactionStyles` (좋아요 · 싫어요 · 공감, the sample server's types), the Korean twin of `defaultBoardReactionStyles`; `apps/sample` and `apps/starter` pass it to the post detail by the device language.
- `BoardLabels.commentCountText` (`String Function(int)`, optional): the whole phrase of a comment count in the post list, so Korean reads `댓글 3` and not `3 댓글`; without it the list keeps `3 ` + `comments`.
- Korean sets `BoardLabels.ko()`, `LegalLabels.ko()` and `ImageUploadLabels.ko()` (polite 해요 style, with the Korean refusal reasons of the photo field), each with a test that the set names every field. The English defaults are unchanged. `iap` has no Labels class (its texts are the app's), so it has no Korean set; the sample and the starter hand them by the device language (`context.byLocale`).

Not in the starter: the page that links a provider to a signed-in account.
